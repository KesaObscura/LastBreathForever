**Last Breath Forever 0.1.0**

First test version.

- Shows about how many seconds your current target has left ("~12 s"), live in combat: solo, in a group, in dungeons and on bosses.
- The seconds appear after a few hits, and only for targets that were (almost) full when the fight with them began: no number is better than a wrong one.
- In the open world a target you come back to in the same fight keeps its seconds.
- Settings (`/lbf`): size, "Show from" (only once about 30 s to 10 min or less are left, or always), last seconds in red, the icon, only elites and bosses, position.
- 9 languages: English, Deutsch, Español (EU and AL), Français, Русский, 한국어, Português, 简体中文, 繁體中文.
